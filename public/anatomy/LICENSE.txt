This skeleton-only derivative is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, retaining the
upstream BodyParts3D attribution. Changes: explicitly select the 208 named bone
mesh objects in source-map.json; triangulate loaded base mesh data; bake source
world transforms; convert Z-up to Y-up; strip materials, textures, modifiers,
annotations, definitions and application code; assign a plain ivory material.

No kidney, inner ear organ, nerve, brain, soft tissue, teeth, cartilage, muscle,
landmark overlays, upstream scripts or Wikipedia definitions are included.
Malleus, incus and stapes are skeletal ossicles, not the separately licensed
inner ear organ model. Selection is an explicit source-name bone allowlist,
not export of the entire Skeletal system collection (which contains non-bones).

208 mesh nodes map to 206 catalog IDs: sternum uses three component nodes.
Source labels and geometry have been checked mechanically; these are not
independent clinical/anatomical certification or verified ontology mappings.
The asset is for education and visualization, not medical diagnosis.
